# Codeathon-Assignment-PowerBI
## 📊 Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, products, customers, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that can support business analysis and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare product and category performance
- Analyze sales and profit across different regions
- Understand customer-level sales patterns
- Identify high-margin and profitable products
- Create an interactive and user-friendly dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculations and measures
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPI cards, tables, and slicers

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

This page provides an overall summary of sales performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Total Quantity

**Key Visualizations:**
- Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Top Products by Profit Margin
- Profit Trend Over Time

---

### 2. Product & Category Analysis

This page focuses on product-level and category-level performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Products
- Best Performing Product
- Highest Margin Product
- Most Profitable Product

**Key Analysis:**
- Top Trending Products
- Category-wise Sales & Profit
- Profit Margin by Category
- Top 5 vs Bottom 5 Products
- Product Performance Matrix

---

### 3. Customer & Region Analysis

This page analyzes customer and regional performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Customers
- Top Region by Profit
- Top Region by Sales
- Average Sales per Customer

**Key Analysis:**
- Sales by Region
- Profit Margin by Region
- Profit by Region
- Customer vs Sales
- Customer Distribution by Region

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The **East region** records the highest sales and profit among the regions.
- **Laptop** is one of the strongest products in terms of profitability and has the highest profit margin.
- Overall sales are approximately **$94.93M**, with total profit of around **$5.14M**.
- The overall profit margin is approximately **5.45%**.
- Regional and customer-level analysis shows variations in sales and profitability.
- Some records contain **Unknown** region/category values, indicating opportunities for further data cleaning.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Maintain the strong performance of the East region while improving lower-performing regions.
- Focus on high-margin and highly profitable products.
- Investigate regions with comparatively lower profit margins.
- Improve data completeness by reviewing Unknown region/category records.
- Use customer-level sales and profit patterns for targeted sales strategies.
